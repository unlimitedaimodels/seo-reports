# SEO Reports

A people-first SEO research collection based on official Google Search guidance. The goal is useful, original content—not mass-produced pages or ranking manipulation.

## Reports

1. **Google Search Essentials Explained** — The technical basics that help pages become crawlable and understandable.
2. **People-First SEO in 2026** — How to create content for readers instead of search-engine loopholes.
3. **SEO for Google AI Overviews** — What Google says about eligibility, links, and foundational SEO.
4. **Technical SEO Checklist** — Titles, headings, canonicals, robots rules, internal links, and page experience.
5. **Search Console Measurement Guide** — Queries, impressions, clicks, indexing, and practical diagnosis.
6. **Structured Data and Rich Results** — Choosing supported schemas and validating implementation.
7. **Crawlability and Indexing** — Sitemaps, redirects, status codes, JavaScript rendering, and exclusions.
8. **Brand Search and Entity SEO** — Building clear, consistent information about a real company.
9. **Link Spam and Risky SEO Tactics** — What to avoid when building mentions and backlinks.
10. **Updating Old Content Without Losing Rankings** — Evidence-led refreshes, consolidation, and removal.
